# 04 — With More Time: Priority Quick Wins, 12-Pillar Scaling Roadmap & Empirical Guardrails

Everything in `gifteval-tsfm-prototype` was deliberately scoped to `16.32M` parameters and a single Apple M2 Max laptop (`2.74 GB` compressed subset, `20k` steps per iteration, `[1, 32, 128]` strip canvases) so every data, scaling, masking, and architectural mechanism could be isolated causally in ~1 day. Every residual failure mode surfaced in [docs/03_evaluation_and_error_analysis.md](03_evaluation_and_error_analysis.md) maps directly onto a mathematically vetted scaling upgrade below (omitting mechanisms we previously invalidated empirically, such as uncalibrated first-difference smoothness losses that break quantile properness).

---

## Priority 0 — Immediate Quick Wins Inside This Prototype

Before scaling backbone depth or GPU clusters, four low-risk experiments require minimal code changes on top of `src/tsfm_proto/`:
1. **Multi-Seed Stability Intervals (`3` Seeds):** Re-run `Run 0`, `Run 1`, and `Run 5` across `3` seeds (`42, 137, 2026`) so checkpoint initialization/order variance is separated from task and dataset-family sampling uncertainty.
2. **Horizon Context Preservation (`K_hor <= 16` Inference Budget Check):** For `10S` long rollouts (`H=900`), compare single-pass `K_hor = 29` (`99` context patches = `528.0` min / `8.80` hours) against Chunked `K_hor = 16` (`112` context patches = `597.3` min / `9.96` hours) + heteroscedastic residual calibration, isolating lookback starvation vs. autoregressive drift.
3. **Adaptive Pretraining Temperature & Step Budget (`alpha in {0.25, 0.35, 0.50}`, `40k–60k` Steps):** Evaluate whether extending WSD plateau length + higher shard count (`8` vs `4` shards on large dirs) narrows the gap on `m4_daily` and `Domain: Energy`.
4. **Domain-Aware Post-Hoc Conformal Calibration (`W = 64` Held-Out Residuals):** Apply lightweight split-conformal additive adjustments keyed on context volatility and horizon step `h` while keeping strictly non-negative `+Clamp/Snap` and signed-only `+SymAvg`.

## 12 Vetted Production Scaling Pillars (Equation-Backed)

### Pillar 1: `3:1` Interleaved Gated Delta-Rule Recurrence + Pointwise-Gated Full Attention (`32k+` Context)
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Fixes the `bizitobs_application/10S` (`nMWQL = 1.85`) and `bizitobs_service/10S` (`nMWQL = 1.63`) failures—where raw test contexts `< 24h` (`21.21–22.87h`), `128`-patch context capping (`8.8–9.7h`), `SEASONAL_LAGS` stopping at `S = 96 < 360`, and **`0 / 152` `10S` directories across the entire `878.26 GB` `GiftEvalPretrain` corpus** compound (alongside `10T` diurnal `S=144` lag omission). Expanding to `32k+` steps increases `10S` lookback to **`91+` hours (`3.8` days)** and `5T` lookback (`bizitobs_l2c/5T/long`) to **`113.8` days** while adding high-cadence lag features (`S in {144, 360, 720, 1440}`) for unseen frequencies.
- **Formulation:**
  - Interleave **`3` chunkwise gated delta-rule linear recurrence blocks per `1` exact causal full-attention block** ($\mathcal{O}(L)$ streaming memory) preceded by observation- and segment-masked causal depthwise `Short-Conv1d(k=4, SiLU)`.
  - Use **channel-wise diagonal retention with rank-1 Householder/Kalman innovation erasure (`Kalman-KDA`)**, executed hardware-efficiently via chunkwise parallel blocks (`4` binary Neumann product factors / `3` matrix squarings $\mathbf{T}^2, \mathbf{T}^4, \mathbf{T}^8$ per $16$-patch chunk), with exact missing-patch state freezing whenever $m^{\text{obs}}_{v,p} = 0$:
    $$
    \mathbf{S}_p = \operatorname{Diag}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_p)\,\mathbf{S}_{p-1} + \beta_p\,\mathbf{k}_p\!\left(\mathbf{v}_p^\top - \mathbf{k}_p^\top \operatorname{Diag}(\boldsymbol{\alpha}_p)\,\mathbf{S}_{p-1}\right), \qquad (\boldsymbol{\alpha}_p, \beta_p) = (\mathbf{1}, 0) \text{ whenever } m^{\text{obs}}_{v,p} = 0.
    $$
  - On every 4th full-attention layer, pair **`HSTU`-style pointwise gated `SiLU` ($\operatorname{SiLU}(\mathbf{Q}\mathbf{K}^\top/\sqrt{d_h})/\sqrt{L_{\text{eff}}}$)** or `QK-Norm + Logit Soft-Cap` with **Origin-Anchored Partial `XPOS` ($\rho = 0.5$)** (plus continuous physical-time **$\Delta t$ rotary embeddings** when generalizing to irregularly sampled event telemetry) to eliminate attention-sink concentration over `2,048–8,192` patch spans.
  - Shift cross-variate `GroupAttention` earlier (`layers 3, L - 2`) and **prune historical-only covariate rows after layer `L - 2`**, saving `~45%` of remaining post-fusion layer FLOPs on covariate-heavy panels ($V_x \approx V_y$, i.e. final layer $L - 1$ at $L = 8$, or tail layers $L - k_{\text{tail}} \dots L - 1$ in deeper `24–32`-layer backbones) without removing any causal path into target horizons.

### Pillar 2: Known-Future Covariates, Gated Decoy Suppression & Calendar Rate Normalization
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Calendar cycles (`us_births/M/short`), holidays, pricing/promotions, and scheduled maintenance (`fev-bench` covariate tasks) are *known* over $+1 \dots +K_{\text{hor}}$ at inference, whereas this prototype only ingests `past_feat_dynamic_real`. Furthermore, standard `Softmax` weights sum to `1` and cannot zero out pure-noise decoy covariates, and extensive monthly counts distort across `28/29/30/31`-day months (`us_births/M/short = 1.052`).
- **Formulation:**
  - Partition stream rows into three explicit variate roles: `target` ($\mathbf{y}$), `historical-only covariate` ($\mathbf{x}$), and `known-future covariate` ($\mathbf{z}$).
  - Run **bidirectional forward+reverse temporal recurrence** on $\mathbf{z}$ tokens, relax causal temporal masking strictly when `role[q] == future_cov` (preserving causality on $\mathbf{y}$ and $\mathbf{x}$), roll one-patch lookahead summaries $\tilde{\mathbf{z}}_{v,p+1}$ onto `role == future_cov` ($\mathbf{z}$) rows (Channel 5) for target rows to read via `GroupAttention`, and enforce the monotonic **asymmetric variate firewall** $\operatorname{role}(q) \le \operatorname{role}(kv)$ under $\operatorname{role} \in \{\mathbf{y}{=}0, \mathbf{x}{=}1, \mathbf{z}{=}2\}$ ($\mathbf{y} \to \{\mathbf{y}, \mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}\}$, $\mathbf{x} \to \{\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}\}$, $\mathbf{z} \to \{\mathbf{z}\}$, i.e. $\neg(\operatorname{role}(q) = \mathbf{z} \wedge \operatorname{role}(kv) \neq \mathbf{z}) \wedge \neg(\operatorname{role}(q) \in \{\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{z}\} \wedge \operatorname{role}(kv) = \mathbf{y})$, so $\mathbf{x}$ can read known $\mathbf{z}$ while neither $\mathbf{x}$ nor $\mathbf{z}$ ever reads target $\mathbf{y}$).
  - Add a **`2 * sigmoid(W_g h)` multiplicative decoy-suppression output gate** (and optional sparse $\alpha$-`entmax` variate attention) inside `GroupAttention` so noise/uninformative channels are silenced directly to `0` despite Softmax conservation.
  - Normalize $\mathbf{z}$ strictly using historical context $1:T$ moments (with bounded fallback when constant on $1:T$), **bypass `arcsinh` compression on `{0, ±1}` binary holiday/promotion flags**, preserve non-zero static level constants ($\sigma_v = 0 \implies \sinh^{-1}(c_v)$), and apply **extensive-to-intensive calendar-duration rate normalization ($y_t / \Delta t_t$ before forecasting, multiplied back by exact future $\Delta t_{T+h}$)** to remove deterministic month-length artifacts.

### Pillar 3: `u-µP`-Safe Multi-Scale Sub-Patch Stem (`4 × 8`-Step Residual Branch)
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** A single $P=32$ linear patch projection smears sharp intra-patch spikes, changepoints, `5T` `S=12` (`0.375P`, `bizitobs_l2c/5T/long`), and low-sample monthly calendar transitions (`us_births/M/short`) without needing a `4x` increase in sequence length.
- **Formulation:** Partition each `32`-step patch into `4` non-overlapping `8`-step sub-patches $[\mathbf{u}_{p,1}, \mathbf{u}_{p,2}, \mathbf{u}_{p,3}, \mathbf{u}_{p,4}]$, apply a shared two-layer intra-patch `SiLU` MLP (`8*C_in -> d/2 -> d/4` per sub-patch, concatenated and linearly pooled via $\mathbf{W}_{\text{pool}}$), normalize via **`u-µP`-safe parameter-free `RMSNorm_0`**, add onto the full-patch `ResBlock` skip path via a **small-init scale-matched gate $\sigma_{\text{base}}\gamma_{\text{sub}}$**, and finish with post-stem `RMSNorm`.

### Pillar 4: Cancellation-Free Björck Local-Scale Statistics, Positive-Skew Transforms & Origin-Blended Readout Frame
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Our prototype computes one frozen global $(\mu_v, \sigma_v)$ and one trailing-64-observed channel (`ch 4`) anchored at $T_{\text{origin}}$. Over `4,096–32,768`-step trajectories, earlier patches $p \ll 0$ lack local normalization anchored at $p$; naive single-pass `E[X^2] - E[X]^2` variances suffer catastrophic cancellation when $|\mu|/\sigma > 10^4$; and non-stationary random-walk drifts (`m4_daily/D/short = 1.208`) or strictly positive exponential series (`electricity`, counts) saturate `arcsinh` when decoded relative to a distant global context mean $\mu^{\text{glob}}_v$.
- **Formulation:**
  - Compute causal rolling statistics $(\mu_{v,p}^{(64)}, \sigma_{v,p}^{(64)})$ strictly over trailing `64` **observed** points via **cancellation-free two-pass centered Björck variance** (with `<8`-observation first-patch backward anchor fill) and exact relative-scale equivariance, apply optional thresholded closed-form OLS linear detrending ($\sigma_{\text{detrend}} < 0.5\,\sigma_{\text{orig}}$) + advancing Welford horizon frames on trended/heteroskedastic series, and ablate monotone positive-skew **`log1p` / Box-Cox dual-transform channels** alongside `arcsinh` on strictly non-negative multiplicative streams.
  - Anchor the quantile head's inverse coordinate frame around an **origin-blended / local `W = 64` readout frame** ($\lambda_{\text{loc}} = \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{1}\{n_w \ge 8\}$) paired with a bounded scale envelope so flat $W = 64$ troughs never blow up inverse $\sinh$:
    $$
    \mu_{v,T}^{\text{dec}} = \lambda_{\text{loc}}\,\mu_{v,T}^{(64)} + (1 - \lambda_{\text{loc}})\,\mu_{v,T}^{\text{glob}}, \qquad \sigma_{v,T}^{\text{dec}} = \max\!\bigl(\sigma_{v,T}^{\text{glob}},\, \mathbf{1}\{n_w \ge 8\}\sigma_{v,T}^{(64)},\, \epsilon_\sigma,\, 10^{-6}|\mu_{v,T}^{\text{dec}}|\bigr),
    $$
    keeping local level shifts sharp near $y_T$ for drifted random walks while preserving global scale stability.

### Pillar 5: Quadrature-Weighted Monotone `Spline-IQF`, Extreme Tails & Coherent Joint Sums
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Discrete `Q21` heads treat unequal-spaced quantile knots identically in loss weighting, interpolate linearly across knots without tail extrapolation beyond `q01 / q99`, and yield only *marginal* step-by-step intervals (whereas inventory/finance risk queries $\sum_{h=1}^L Y_{T+h}$ require joint correlation across steps—*"the P90 of a sum is not the sum of the P90s"*).
- **Formulation:**
  - Weight arbitrary quantile knots cleanly via exact **trapezoidal midpoint quadrature** $w_{q_k} = \frac{1}{2}(q_{k+1} - q_{k-1})$ for unequal tail grids (interior knots; unit-interval boundary closure $b_0=0, b_Q=1$ giving $w_{q_1}=\frac{q_1+q_2}{2}$ and $w_{q_Q}=1-\frac{q_{Q-1}+q_Q}{2}$).
  - Upgrade discrete knots to a $C^1$ monotonic rational-quadratic **Gregory-Delbourgo `Spline-IQF`** with differentiable **Student-$t(\nu = 3)$ / Generalized Pareto (GPD)** extreme-tail extensions beyond $[0.01, 0.99]$ (or pair with an optional **`1-NFE` Conditional Flow Matching (`MMDiT`, $m = 32$ joint particles)** head for multimodal trajectories).
  - Aggregate joint multi-step sums $S = \sum_{h=1}^L Y_{T+h}$ cleanly via a **lightweight shrinkage Gaussian / empirical rank copula** across horizon dependencies combined with **coherent `MinT` linear hierarchical reconciliation** when forecasting aggregate totals alongside constituent sub-series.

### Pillar 6: Single-Pass Direct `H <= 1,024` + `5`-Anchor Quadrature Reserved for `H > 1,024`
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Two-chunk autoregressive rollouts (`Runs 0..1` at `H > 512`) propagate only the Chunk-1 median (`q50`), under-representing branching uncertainty on Chunk 2.
- **Formulation:** Keep **single-pass parallel masked decoding for all horizons $H \le 1,024$ (`32` patches)** once context is unstarved (`L >= 2,048` steps, eliminating autoregressive compounding error). Reserve **`5`-anchor `{0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 0.90}` quadrature trajectories** (`quadrature weights = {0.175, 0.200, 0.250, 0.200, 0.175}`) strictly for **ultra-long rollouts $H > 1,024$**, conditioning Chunk 2 independently across all `5` paths and mixing their predictive CDFs, and add **multi-stride shifted-origin / multi-checkpoint test-time ensembling** (`3–5` fractional phase offsets when compute budget permits) to cut $S \bmod P \ne 0$ boundary variance.

### Pillar 7: Supply-Chain Censored Demand (`Tobit`), Zero-Preserving `TSMixup`, Decoys & Bounded Dynamics
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Real `GiftEvalPretrain` under-represents censored retail stockouts, intermittent sparse demands (`car_parts_with_missing`), cold-start launches, volatility clustering, and high-noise sensor decoys (`50%` zero-MI distractors for `GroupAttention`).
- **Formulation:** Combine `5` complementary synthetic generators across a `3`-stage curriculum:
  1. `TabICL`-style **Structural Causal Model (SCM) DAGs** with **mandatory `50%` zero-Mutual-Information decoy covariates** (training the Pillar-2 `2 * sigmoid` gate to suppress noise) plus **`5%` cohort cold-start launches** pairing `3–10`-step targets with long mature peer curves.
  2. **Retail `(s, S)` stockout right-censoring ($S_t = \min(D_t, I_t)$)** paired with an observed inventory cap $\tilde{I}_t = \sinh^{-1}\!\bigl((I_t - \mu)/\sigma\bigr)$ via **Powell (1986) Tobit right-censored quantile loss** $\ell^{\text{Tobit}}_q(\tilde{y}^{\text{obs}}, \hat{\tilde{y}}^q; \tilde{I}_t) = \rho_q\!\bigl(\tilde{y}^{\text{obs}} - \min(\hat{\tilde{y}}^q, \tilde{I}_t)\bigr)$—clamping predictions inside $\min(\cdot, \tilde{I}_t)$ zeros the gradient with respect to $\hat{\tilde{y}}^q$ strictly whenever $\hat{\tilde{y}}^q > \tilde{I}_t$ ($\hat{y}^q > I_t$, whether $S_t = I_t < \hat{y}^q$ or $D_t < I_t < \hat{y}^q$, while preserving upward gradient $-q$ when $\hat{y}^q < S_t = I_t$; on stockout steps `stockout_mask == 1` where $\tilde{y}^{\text{obs}} = \tilde{I}_t$, this applies a one-sided floor penalty $q\max(0, \tilde{I}_t - \hat{\tilde{y}}^q)$, where raw zero inventory $I_t = 0$ maps to $\tilde{I}_t = \sinh^{-1}(-\mu/\sigma)$, equaling $0$ when $\mu = 0$), teaching latent true demand without fitting truncated empty-shelf caps.
  3. **Zero-preserving intermittent `TSMixup`** alongside orthogonal **Haar DWT `WaveMixup`** recombined strictly from truncated `train_views`.
  4. **Hurst-calibrated ($\mathcal{H} = d' + \tfrac{1}{2} \in [0.60, 0.96]$, $d' \in [0.10, 0.46]$) bounded Volterra / damped-driven nonlinear oscillators**, **GARCH(1,1)** volatility clustering, and fractional **ARFIMA** long-memory processes.
  5. **Cointegrated `VECM` / coupled `VAR`** multivariate systems, piecewise-regime `KernelSynth`, and Poisson-Gamma counts.

### Pillar 8: Three-Gate Multi-Benchmark Decontamination & Expansion (`GIFT-Eval` + `fev-bench` + `TIME`)
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Eliminates the `4` degenerate two-level `[0, ..., c, ..., 0]` impulse hash collisions observed in [leakage_audit.json](../results/leakage_audit.json) and expands evaluation beyond `GIFT-Eval`.
- **Formulation:** Enforce three strict gates across `GIFT-Eval` (`97` configs), `fev-bench` (`100` tasks, `42` with rich dynamic covariates), and post-cutoff `TIME` (`98` tasks):
  1. Normalized family/alias exclusion (`weather` Monash/`oikolab` $\neq$ `jena_weather`).
  2. **Non-degenerate support filter (`n_unique >= 8`) + z-normalized Pearson similarity ($r < 0.99$)** on levels **and first differences ($\Delta y$)** so constant/impulse plateaus cannot trigger false positives while true sub-series leaks are caught.
  3. Sliding-window temporal holdout truncation.

### Pillar 9: 2D Axial BFD Rectangle Packing vs. 1D Jagged Packing + `FlexAttention`
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Fixed `[1, 32, 128]` grids work cleanly at laptop batch sizes (`~99.6%` occupancy here), whereas multi-node `50B+`-token pretraining mixes `V=37+` multivariate panels, `V_x >> V_y` covariate panels, and lengths spanning `N = 4..1024` patches.
- **Formulation:** Partition irregular tasks using the packing regime matched to workload structure:
  - **2D Axial Best-Fit-Decreasing (BFD) Rectangle Bin-Packing (`[V_pack × N_pack]`):** Packs aligned multi-target panels (`>98.5%` density) while permitting zero-copy 2D transpose between temporal and cross-variate attention axes.
  - **1D Ragged Jagged Sequence Packing + `torch.compile` Block-Sparse `FlexAttention`:** When historical-only covariates $\mathbf{x}$ greatly outnumber target series ($V_x \gg V_y$), pack variable-length streams into 1D jagged tensors and **truncate $\mathbf{x}$ tokens strictly at $T_{\text{origin}}$** so zero horizon compute/memory is wasted on masked future covariate slots, compiling static-capacity block masks with zero shape recompilations.

### Pillar 10: `u-µP` + `NorMuon` + Update-Sign Cautious Weight Decay (`CWD`) + `OnlineEMA`
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Transferring learning rates and weight-decay hyperparameters tuned at `~16M` width zero-shot to `300M–1B+` multi-node runs without re-sweeping.
- **Formulation:**
  - Pair **Unit-Scaled Maximal Update Parameterization (`u-µP`)** and **pre-`RMSNorm`** (or optional `TimesFM 3.0`-style **`Sandwich RMSNorm`** adding post-sublayer `RMSNorm` with matching residual branch output scaling to preserve `u-µP` variance propagation) with **Neuron-wise Normalized Muon (`NorMuon`)** on 2D+ hidden matrices (`5`-step quintic Newton-Schulz matrix orthogonalization followed by neuron-wise second-moment RMS normalization—per-row when $d_{\text{out}} \ge d_{\text{in}}$, per-column otherwise).
  - Apply **Cautious Weight Decay (`CWD`) gated by sign agreement between the orthogonalized update $\mathbf{U}_n$ and parameter matrix $\mathbf{W}_{n-1}$** ($\mathbf{1}[\mathbf{U}_n \odot \mathbf{W}_{n-1} > 0]$), while enforcing **`weight_decay = 0.0` on all 1D norms, biases, embeddings, stems, and readout `AdamW` groups**.
  - Maintain **`OnlineEMA` shadow parameters** alongside WSD cooldown souping to combine smooth trajectory averaging without early-cooldown LR pollution.

### Pillar 11: Dense Multi-Origin Causal Next-Patch Supervision & Hybrid `70/30` `CPM`
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Pure suffix supervision on a packed `128`-patch strip only supervises future horizon tokens (`1..32` patches), leaving up to `127` historical context tokens without direct quantile-head gradients! *(Note: we explicitly reject auxiliary first-difference smoothness penalties across adjacent horizon patches because $Q_{\Delta Y}(q) \neq Q_{Y_{t+1}}(q) - Q_{Y_t}(q)$ violates strict quantile properness and degrades CRPS calibration.)*
- **Formulation:**
  - Add **Dense Multi-Origin Causal Next-Patch Supervision** under pure strictly-proper CRPS: score intermediate context patches causally using past-only context—or deploy two-stream **Decoupled Horizon Forecasting (`DHF` `[Enc ; Dec]`)** inside `1D` jagged packing (`~25%` lower attention FLOPs in single-origin two-stream mode vs. concatenated single-horizon SDPA by skipping the empty `Enc -> Dec` upper-right block, or restricted to a lightweight top-layer readout for multi-origin `DHF` since full-depth multi-origin `DHF` inflates FLOPs relative to `2.25–4.0x` leaner in-place **`Hybrid 70/30 Contiguous Patch Masking (CPM)`** (`70%` suffix horizons of `1..32` patches + `30%` interior span prediction of `1..8` patches))—and **`p = 0.15` random non-target variate dropout**.
  - Optionally overlap adjacent horizon readout windows with **`2P = 64` half-stride linear cross-fading** to eliminate patch-boundary seams cleanly without distorting quantile properness.

### Pillar 12: Dual-Cadence Low-Res Stems, Retrieval-Augmented Peers (`RAF`) & Rank-8 `LoRA`
- **Empirical Bottleneck Solved:** Low-frequency `A/Q/M/W` tasks and new-product cold starts often contain only `3–48` total observations (`1–2` coarse `P=32` patches; `Run 5` `Low-Res Freq A/Q/M/W, n=15`: `0.7451` raw / `0.7408` `+SymAvg+Clamp` [`0.7397` raw in `Run 4`] vs. `0.5706` raw / `0.5649` `+SymAvg+Clamp` on `High-Res Freq, n=82`).
- **Formulation:**
  - Route short/low-frequency inputs to finer **`P = 16` / `P = 8` dual-cadence stem adapters** sharing Transformer backbone weights with `P = 32` via lightweight linear adapters, doubling/quadrupling (`2x` / `4x`) patch resolution on yearly, quarterly, and monthly horizons.
  - Pair cold-start streams (`3–10` observations) with **Retrieval-Augmented Forecasting (`RAF`)**, injecting top-$k$ historical analog peers directly as read-only reference rows inside `GroupAttention` alongside **causal empirical-Bayes target-encoded static categorical embeddings** (store/SKU hierarchies) and **rank-8 `LoRA` tenant adaptation** for domain specialization without catastrophic forgetting.

## 6 Empirical Guardrails (What We Explicitly Tested & Will NOT Repeat)

1. **Never use a fixed additive variance floor ($\sqrt{\sigma^2 + \epsilon^2}$)** on raw SI-unit series (`era5` $\sim 10^{-6}$, `bitbrains` $\sim 6\times 10^{-4}$); always use relative scale tolerance (`1.50e-15` [`< 2e-15`] on `NB01`'s micro-SI test and $\mathcal{O}((|\mu|/\sigma)\epsilon_{\text{f64}})$ generally in `float64` scalar moments/`arcsinh`; `0.0` on exact powers of two and `< 5e-5` across tested decimal SI rescalings [`~9e-7` in `NB01`, `1.32e-5` in `test_causality_and_masks.py`] in `float32`-cast `build_group_patch_features`, whenever both rescaled series remain strictly above the `1e-12` underflow floor).
2. **Never apply unconstrained sign-reflection (`+SymAvg`)** to non-negative right-skewed datasets (`solar`, `electricity`, counts); restrict strictly to signed windows with `(fin < 0.0).any()`.
3. **Never rely on Q/K-only `RoPE` alone** without `rel_origin_pos` (`ch 3`); `RoPE` does not rotate `V`, so `rel_origin_pos` is needed to disambiguate Layer-0 residual horizon slots `+1..+K` on 1-patch short series.
4. **Never expose zeroed future past-covariate placeholders (`is_hor & icov`)** as softmax keys inside cross-variate attention (`key_ok = obs | (is_hor & (~icov))`).
5. **Never exclude blank keys in causal masks without `eye_l` self-diagonal fallback** (completely missing `100%`-NaN cropped windows produce `0/0 = NaN` in SDPA otherwise).
6. **Never launch cross-variate `GroupAttention` from Step 0 at `1.0x` backbone LR** or on real-only `past_cov` sparsity (`8.5%` series across `13/93` dirs); warm up after temporal stabilization and co-train on coupled synthetic drivers (`>10%` coupled mix).
